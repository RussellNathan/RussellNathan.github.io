Transfiguration

(2023)

“a complete change of form or appearance into a more beautiful or spiritual state.”

Social media and technology is undeniably transforming our perception of ourselves, in what could be described as a rotoscopic revolution. AR filters, like what you see on Snapchat and Instagram, are a key foundation in that.

The experience was designed for mobile with Meta's Spark AR Studio and can be found here.
The controls are as follows:

● Opening your hand increases the frequency of the high pass filter of a microtonal synth
● Vertical movement controls the pitch
● Horizontal movement will trigger percussive sounds, dynamically reacting to the velocity at which the hand moves.
Opening the mouth distorts the audio, creating more chaos and noise.
